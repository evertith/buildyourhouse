### [How to Choose Land for Building: What They Don't Tell You Until It's Too Late](/blog/how-to-choose-land-for-building)

**That perfect piece of land might be a $50,000 nightmare. Here's how to tell the difference.**

Beautiful views. Perfect location. Great price. You're ready to make an offer. But did you check the soil report? The septic suitability? The actual cost to run power? The buildable area after setbacks? This post could save you from the most expensive mistake you'll ever make.

*What you'll learn*:
- The 27-point due diligence checklist (print it, use it)
- Red flags that mean "walk away no matter how good it looks"
- Hidden costs that turn "$50K for land" into "$120K before you pour concrete"
- How to read a survey and why most people miss the critical details

**Read time: 14 minutes**

### [Managing a Construction Loan as an Owner-Builder](/blog/managing-construction-loan-as-owner-builder)

**Getting the money is hard. Not screwing up the draw schedule is harder.**

Construction loans aren't like regular mortgages. Miss a draw deadline, and you're funding the next phase out of pocket. Fail an inspection, and the bank won't release funds. Have the wrong documentation, and you'll wait weeks for money you need now. This is the guide to not imploding your construction loan.

*What you'll learn*:
- Which loan types actually work for owner-builders (most don't)
- How draw schedules really work (and why timing is everything)
- The documentation the bank wants before they'll release a dime
- What to do when the inspector fails something and your draw is frozen

**Read time: 13 minutes**

### [Is Owner-Building Right During a Recession? Economic Timing vs. Life Timing](/blog/is-owner-building-right-recession)

**The economy is weird. Interest rates are high. Material costs are volatile. Should you wait?**

Everyone's got an opinion about market timing. "Wait for rates to drop." "Material costs will come down." "Now is the worst time." But what if you're 35 with two kids in a 900 sq ft apartment? What if you're 50 and tired of waiting for the "right time"? This post cuts through the noise.

*What you'll learn*:
- Economic factors that actually matter (and which ones are just noise)
- Why waiting for "perfect conditions" often costs more than building now
- How to build recession-proof into your plans
- The case for building when everyone says you shouldn't

**Read time: 11 minutes**
