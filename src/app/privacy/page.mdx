## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

### How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies for:

- **Essential Functions** - Remembering your preferences, maintaining sessions
- **Analytics** - Understanding how visitors use our site (via Google Analytics or similar)
- **Advertising** - Serving and measuring ads through Mediavine, including interest-based ads unless you opt out
- **Performance** - Identifying and fixing technical issues
- **Functionality** - Providing enhanced features and personalization

### Types of Cookies We Use

**Strictly Necessary Cookies**
Required for the website to function. Cannot be disabled.

**Analytics Cookies**
Help us understand how visitors interact with our site by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

**Functional Cookies**
Enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences.

**Advertising Cookies**
Set by our advertising partner, Mediavine, and its partners to serve ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure performance, and, unless you opt out, personalize ads to your interests. The Mediavine Advertising Privacy Notice at the end of this policy lists the opt-out tools.

### Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies
- Delete cookies after each session
- Be notified when cookies are set

**Note:** Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

### Third-Party Analytics

We use **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as:

- How long you stay on our site
- Which pages you visit
- Where you came from (referring site)
- Your approximate location (city/region)
- Device and browser information

This information is collected anonymously and reported in aggregate. Google may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy.

**Opt-out:** You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

We also use **Microsoft Clarity** to understand how visitors move through our pages, through heatmaps and anonymized session replays. We use it only to improve the site. Microsoft's handling of this data is described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The browser cookie controls above apply to Clarity as well.
