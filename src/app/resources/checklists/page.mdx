## Using These Checklists Effectively

### Print and Organize
Print each relevant checklist and keep them in a job site binder. As you complete items, check them off and note the date. This documentation proves invaluable for:
- Tracking progress
- Identifying delays early
- Preparing for inspections
- Resolving disputes
- Creating your project record

### Customize for Your Project
These checklists are comprehensive starting points. Add items specific to your project, local code requirements, or personal preferences. Remove items that don't apply to keep the lists manageable.

### Schedule Regular Reviews
Review applicable checklists weekly during project planning and daily during active construction. This habit prevents surprises and keeps you ahead of the next phase.
