\section*{PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) REQUIREMENTS}

\textbf{REQUIRED at all times on site:}

$\square$ Hard hat in designated areas or when overhead work is being performed

$\square$ Safety glasses or goggles when operating power tools or in designated areas

$\square$ Work boots with slip-resistant soles (no tennis shoes or sandals)

$\square$ Long pants (no shorts)

$\square$ High-visibility vest (when required by site conditions)

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{REQUIRED for specific tasks:}

$\square$ Hearing protection when noise levels exceed 85 decibels

$\square$ Respirator or dust mask when working with:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.75in]
\item Insulation materials
\item Drywall dust/sanding
\item Spray paint or coatings
\item Demolition work
\item Other airborne contaminants
\end{itemize}

$\square$ Gloves appropriate for the task (cut-resistant, chemical-resistant, etc.)

$\square$ Fall protection harness when working above 6 feet

$\square$ Knee pads for floor work

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{PPE Availability:}

Owner will provide basic PPE: $\square$ Yes $\square$ No

Location of PPE on site: \rule{7cm}{0.4pt}

\section*{FALL PROTECTION}

\textbf{Fall protection is REQUIRED when working:}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.5in]
\item 6 feet or more above ground level
\item On scaffolding
\item On roof surfaces
\item Near unprotected edges or openings
\item On ladders above 6 feet for extended periods
\end{itemize}

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Fall Protection Measures:}

$\square$ Guardrails installed around floor openings and edges

$\square$ Safety harness and lanyard system available

$\square$ Safety nets (if applicable)

$\square$ Warning line systems

$\square$ Hole covers clearly marked

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Scaffolding Requirements:}

$\square$ Erected by qualified personnel

$\square$ Inspected before each use

$\square$ Equipped with guardrails and toe boards

$\square$ On stable, level surface

$\square$ Tagged with inspection date

\vspace{12pt}

\section*{LADDER SAFETY}

\textbf{All ladders must:}

$\square$ Be in good condition (no broken rungs, damaged rails, or missing parts)

$\square$ Be properly rated for the load (minimum Type II - 225 lbs)

$\square$ Be placed on stable, level surface

$\square$ Be secured at top or have someone foot the ladder

$\square$ Extend at least 3 feet above landing when used for roof access

$\square$ Be set at proper angle (4:1 ratio - 1 foot out for every 4 feet up)

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Ladder Use Rules:}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.5in]
\item Maintain three points of contact at all times
\item Face ladder when climbing up or down
\item Do not stand on top two rungs
\item Do not overreach - move ladder instead
\item Never use metal ladders near electrical work
\item Inspect ladder before each use
\end{itemize}

\section*{POWER TOOL \& EQUIPMENT SAFETY}

\textbf{General Requirements:}

$\square$ All power tools must have guards in place and functioning

$\square$ Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter (GFCI) protection required for all electrical tools

$\square$ Extension cords must be 12-gauge or heavier, in good condition

$\square$ Tools must be inspected before each use

$\square$ Only trained operators may use power tools

$\square$ Tools must be unplugged when changing blades, bits, or attachments

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Specific Tool Safety:}

\textbf{Circular Saws:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.75in]
\item Lower guard must be functional
\item Unplug before blade changes
\item Secure work piece before cutting
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Table Saws:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.75in]
\item Blade guard in place
\item Use push sticks for narrow cuts
\item Stand to side, never directly behind blade
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Nail Guns:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.75in]
\item Sequential trigger preferred over bump fire
\item Never point at anyone
\item Disconnect air supply before clearing jams
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Powder-Actuated Tools:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.75in]
\item Operator certification required
\item Never use on brittle materials
\item Hearing and eye protection mandatory
\end{itemize}

\vspace{12pt}

\section*{TRENCHING \& EXCAVATION SAFETY}

\textbf{Required for trenches 4 feet or deeper:}

$\square$ Protective system (shoring, shielding, or sloping)

$\square$ Competent person on site for daily inspections

$\square$ Ladders or ramps within 25 feet of workers

$\square$ Soil and adjacent areas inspected for indications of possible cave-in

$\square$ Utilities located and marked before digging (call 811)

$\square$ Spoil piles kept at least 2 feet from edge

\textbf{Never enter a trench without proper protection!}

\section*{INCIDENT REPORTING}

\item Notify project owner immediately
\item Complete incident report form within 24 hours
\item Document scene with photos (if safe to do so)
\item Preserve evidence and interview witnesses
\item Identify root cause and corrective actions

\section*{LIABILITY \& ACKNOWLEDGMENT}

\textbf{Contractor/Subcontractor Responsibilities:}

Each contractor and subcontractor agrees that they:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.5in]
\item Are responsible for the safety of their own employees
\item Will comply with all safety requirements outlined in this document
\item Will provide appropriate PPE to their employees
\item Maintain required insurance coverage
\item Will report all incidents and injuries immediately
\item Are responsible for any OSHA violations by their employees
\item Will conduct their own safety training and toolbox talks
\end{itemize}

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Right to Stop Work:}

Any person has the right and responsibility to stop work if they observe unsafe conditions or practices. Work shall not resume until the hazard is corrected.

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Violation of Safety Requirements:}

Failure to comply with these safety requirements may result in:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.75in]
\item Immediate removal from job site
\item Termination of contract
\item Withholding of payment
\end{itemize}
