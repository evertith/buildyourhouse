\section*{DISPUTE RESOLUTION}

Any disputes arising under this agreement shall be resolved as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Direct Communication:} Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through direct communication within 7 days of dispute arising.

\item \textbf{Written Notice:} If dispute is not resolved, the aggrieved party shall provide written notice detailing the dispute within 14 days.

\item \textbf{Meeting:} Parties shall meet in person within 14 days of written notice to attempt resolution.

\item \textbf{Mediation:} If meeting does not resolve dispute, parties agree to participate in mediation with a mutually agreed upon mediator. Cost of mediation shall be split equally.

\item \textbf{Arbitration/Litigation:} If mediation fails, parties may pursue:

   $\square$ Binding arbitration (check if selected)

   $\square$ Litigation in courts of \rule{4cm}{0.4pt} County, \rule{2cm}{0.4pt} (State)
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Termination:}

\textbf{By Owner:} Owner may terminate this agreement for cause (failure to perform, safety violations, code violations) with 7 days written notice. Owner may terminate without cause with 14 days written notice.

\textbf{By Subcontractor:} Subcontractor may terminate for non-payment after providing 14 days written notice and opportunity to cure.

Upon termination, Subcontractor shall be paid for all work completed to date, less any costs incurred by Owner to correct deficiencies.

\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Indemnification:}

Subcontractor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Owner from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including attorney fees) arising from:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.5in]
\item Subcontractor's negligent acts or omissions
\item Injury to persons or property caused by Subcontractor or its employees
\item Failure to comply with applicable laws and regulations
\item Claims by Subcontractor's employees, suppliers, or sub-subcontractors
\end{itemize}
